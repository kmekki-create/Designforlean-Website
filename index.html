HOW IT WORKS

From enrollment to certified in four steps

Designed around a working schedule. Most Green Belt learners study 3–4 hours a week.

Pick your starting belt

Take our 2-minute placement guide or talk to an advisor to land on the right level for your role.

Learn the method

Work through short, focused modules covering Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, and Control (DMAIC).

Run a real project

Apply the method to an actual process at your own organization, with async feedback from your mentor.

Certify and present

Present your capstone to a certified Master Black Belt for review, then receive your certification and digital badge.
