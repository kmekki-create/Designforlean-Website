PROGRAMS

Four belts. One method. Zero fluff.

Every program follows the same DMAIC method — Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, Control — at increasing depth. Pick the level that matches what your role actually needs.

White Belt

The vocabulary and mindset behind Lean Six Sigma. No prerequisite.

  • 4 hours, fully self-paced
  • Core DMAIC vocabulary
  • Digital badge on completion
  • No capstone required
